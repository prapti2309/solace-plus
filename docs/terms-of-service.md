- A licensed medical service

### 6.2 AI Limitations

The AI system powering Solace+:

- Is a general-purpose large language model adapted for wellness conversations
- Can make errors, misunderstand context, or produce inaccurate information
- Does not have medical training or clinical judgment

No response from the Solace+ AI should be treated as professional medical advice, a clinical assessment, or a diagnosis.

If you are experiencing symptoms of a mental health condition, please seek support from a qualified licensed mental health professional. Solace+ can complement — but cannot replace — professional care.
